Should I enhance my cognition?
Cognitive enhancement is the practice of empowering your body, and therefore your brain to perform at the best of their ability to suit your neurological goals.
While there are chemical interventions we can implement to help us achieve this enhancement, we have to ensure our bodies and brains are properly equipped to get the most out of the interventions we implement.
What does that look like?
Our lifestyles have a direct effect on the way our brains work. To best cater to this, we have to focus on leveraging lifestyle factors like diet, sleep, and exercise to best suit our goals.
If you are neglecting these factors, most chemical interventions will have a substantially reduced effect.
Lifestyle Factors
Sleep Basics
Sleep is immensely important for your attention, focus, memory consolidation, emotional processing, BDNF secretion, and synaptic pruning.
For basic sleep hygiene,
DO
Wake and go to sleep at the same time daily ±30min
Sunlight exposure within 30 minutes of waking
Reduce bright light exposure 90-120 mins before bed
Reduce room temp (65-67 degrees fahrenheit)
Get 7-9 hours nightly
DO NOT
Vary your wake times
Limit bright screen use 1-2hrs before bed
Have caffeine >8 hours before bed
Have large meals >3 hours before bed
Stay in bed if you can not sleep
Diet Basics
How to:
Stabilize blood sugar
Balance your meals by pairing protein sources with fat and fiber rich carbs at every meal
Avoid carbs without protein or fats
Time heavier carb intakes to AFTER mental/physical exertion
Reduce diet-induced inflammation
Prioritize anti-inflammatory foods (Berries, Leafy Greens, Fish, Black Pepper etc.)
Optimize Omega-6 to Omega-3 ratio by supplementing EPA/DHA
Identify food sensitivities
Micronutrient Density
Supplement key nutrients if diet is deficient (Magnesium, Zinc, B-complex, Vitamin D2+K2)
Eat leafy greens (uncooked) with 1-2 meals a day
Diversify all foods, do not repeat the same meal and carb/protein/fat sources over and over.
Support the Gut-Brain Axis
Avoid alcohol
Avoid antibiotics unless absolutely necessary
Take a diverse probiotic
Take a prebiotic
Track symptoms of bloating, mood swings, and sugar cravings and adjust meal ingredients/size
Exercise Basics
Cardio 3-5x a week, 15 minutes minimum
Resistance training 2-5x a week
Basic Enhancement Pathways
What is to be gained from elevating BDNF?
Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor, or BDNF, is a protein in the brain that contributes greatly to synaptic plasticity, neuronal growth, and neuronal survival.
What is BDNF doing?
BDNF strengthens the brain’s ability to form and reorganize connections at the synaptic level
Promotes Long-Term Potentiation, the mechanism behind learning
Enhances memory consolidation
What does this mean?
BDNF improves
Learning speed
Memory retention
Problem solving
Attention
Impulse control
Task switching
Mood regulation
How can we use increases in BDNF to our advantage?
BDNF both protects your brain and enhances synaptic plasticity (in essence, your brain’s ability to change on a connection based level.), and through increasing it we can learn much more effectively by refining the connections our brain is making.
Take for example, rewiring what you find to be rewarding/displeasing or further “automating” tasks or systems that you once found to be taxing.
Not only does this synaptic plasticity allow us to learn new things faster, but it allows us to expand on that learning by forming new associative connections in the brain adjacent to what we are learning.

What is to be gained from cholinergics?
Cholinergics are a class of enhancements that enhance the transmission, release, and action of acetylcholine (ACh).
Acetylcholine enhances the Signal-to-Noise ratio in the prefrontal cortex, which means that it is enhancing your active attention and ability to filter out distractions.
Acetylcholine also significantly boosts memory consolidation and emotional processing
Acutely, the enhancement of the cholinergic pathway boosts your processing speed, working memory, and is best implemented as a focus agent

How do we use cerebrolysin intranasally?
Cerebrolysin oxidizes extremely quickly, meaning it quickly loses efficacy once exposed to open air. Because of this, it is best NOT to use cerebrolysin from ampules for intranasal use.
Ampules do not come with less than 5mL per ampule, therefore if you were to have 0.3mL intranasally, you’d be effectively wasting 4.7mL of cerebrolysin.
We can avoid all of this by using the lyophilized cerebrolysin, and only pulling out the exact dose we need once reconstituted.
You would:
Crack the top off the vial
Swab the vial with alcohol
Reconstitute with 2mL (or your desired amount) of BAC water
Pull your desired dose from the vial (average appears to be 0.3mL-0.5mL)
Place insulin syringe tip beneath your nose and slowly inhale as you push the plunger

Reconstitution Guide
Grab your vial of lyophilized powder
Grab your vial of Bacteriostatic water
Grab your 29g-31g insulin syringes
Grab your alcohol prep pads
—Crack the top off of your Bacteriostatic (BAC) water
—Swab the top of the vial with your alcohol pads
—Draw 1mL of BAC water into the syringe
—Crack the top off of your lyophilized powder vial
—Swab the top of the vial with your alcohol pads
—Inject your 1mL of BAC water into the vial SLOWLY, and at an angle so that the water is hitting the vial wall and not the powder.
—Repeat until you have your desired dilution of the powder
—Gently roll the vial back and forth in your hands until ALL of the powder is dissolved
Store non-reconstituted vials in the freezer, and reconstituted vials in the fridge

—You would reconstitute your peptide with 1mL

—You would draw the reconstituted peptide from the vial and dispense into the spray bottle
