CURSOR / CODEX IMPLEMENTATION INSTRUCTIONS

GOAL
Use these approved assets as locked visual masters for hundreds of catalog
products. This is content replacement, not image generation or redesign.

LABEL SELECTION
01 = 3 mL catalog, 40 x 20 mm
02 = 3 mL calculator, 40 x 20 mm
03 = 10 mL catalog, 50 x 30 mm
04 = 10 mL calculator, 50 x 30 mm

SOURCE OF TRUTH
Use labels/*_EDIT_CONTENT_ONLY.svg. The matching *_LOCKED.png is the visual
reference. Do not use AI image generation to write text onto a vial.

EDITABLE CONTENT
- Text nodes identified by data-field.
- BRAND_IMAGE: replace its image content inside the existing locked bounds.
- QR_IMAGE: generate from QR_VALUE or replace the image inside the existing
  square QR bounds.
- MASCOT_IMAGE: replace its image content inside the existing locked bounds.
- BRAND_GAP_CHARS: default 1; allowed 0 through 4.
- QR_ENABLED and MASCOT_ENABLED.

LOCKED BEHAVIOR
- Do not change any label dimensions, viewBox, rail, panel, divider, amount bar,
  legal panel, field coordinates, clipping path, or image bounds.
- Preserve aspect ratio for brand and mascot. Never stretch them.
- Keep QR square, high contrast, and inside its current slot.
- Keep mascot behind and above the QR in the same stacking order.
- Do not add an outside border or white margin.
- Preserve the complete black left rail through the label edge.
- Scale the complete SVG as one unit when placing it on a vial.

VIAL SELECTION
- KLOW, GLOW, GHK-Cu, AHK-Cu -> 3 mL cobalt-blue powder.
- B12 -> 10 mL ruby-red transparent liquid at 75% fill.
- Other 3 mL lyophilized products -> 3 mL white powder.
- Other 10 mL lyophilized products -> 10 mL white powder.

ACCEPTANCE TEST
Render the changed SVG at print resolution and compare it with its locked PNG.
Pixels outside the editable text, brand, QR, and mascot regions must not move.
The entire label must remain inside the approved physical dimensions.

IMPLEMENT THIS DATA CONTRACT
Read EDITABLE_FIELDS.json. Product records supply values; the renderer only
updates matching data-field content and never reconstructs the label.
